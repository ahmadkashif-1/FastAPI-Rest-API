The Meridian brief

How this place works.

A transparent research exchange for sharing useful work, finding context, and asking better questions.

01

Read with context.

Meridian presents published research with its title, author, date, and full text. Select any record to open the complete paper and inspect the researcher behind it.

02

Publish a considered contribution.

Use the Publish workspace for articles, working notes, and research summaries.

03

Build a visible profile.

Every publication is connected to its researcher, making a body of work legible.

04

Use Meridian AI as a second reader.

Ask for framing and next steps, then verify claims and citations before relying on any response.

05

Keep the signal high.

Lead with the question and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
